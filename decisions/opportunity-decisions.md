# Opportunity Decisions

This document records decisions and their evidence status to avoid repeating rejected or unresolved research.

## Project-level decisions

| Decision | Status | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Begin with research and documentation, not immediate implementation | Active | Need to validate providers, economics, and distribution access first |
| Prioritise zero-upfront-capital entry | Active | Initial constraint; avoid fees, inventory, purchases, and mandatory paid certification |
| Investigate network-based distribution, not only individual affiliate referrals | Active | Existing trusted networks may provide more relevant market access |
| Treat Farmonaut agriculture and mining separately | Active | Different products, buyers, use cases, and potentially different programme terms |
| Maintain detailed GitHub records and a structured spreadsheet register | Active | Preserve evidence and make the opportunity portfolio comparable |
| Apply broad-first opportunity discovery across locations and subjects | Active | Avoid allowing an initial sector, location, provider or organisation to become the permanent centre before comparative evidence exists |
| Treat Plateau agriculture as one pathway within the parent mission | Active | Use the Plateau research to understand a wider economic and institutional environment, not only maize or formal cooperatives |
| Investigate existing solutions before considering a build | Active | Prefer viable existing providers, partnerships, integration, implementation or distribution; build only after a genuine gap and concrete incentive are established |

## Research architecture decisions

- The mission is broad opportunity discovery and commercial intelligence, not solely technology affiliate or distribution research.
- Research pathways such as Farmonaut, agriculture, mining and Plateau are provisional applications of the parent mission, not permanent boundaries.
- Broad-first exploration and comparative review are the default before narrow commitment. A justified urgent or unusually promising deep dive is allowed when its reason is recorded.
- Dated underlying research records and decision records establish continuity. A manually maintained current-state or handover summary is not authoritative.
- No outreach, payment, registration, pilot commitment or external representation is authorised by research documentation alone.

## Decision rule

A lead becomes a qualified opportunity only when the relevant provider's commercial requirements, market suitability, and permitted distribution route have been evidenced. A network becomes an active partner only after the organisation or its authorised representative has agreed to a defined role.
