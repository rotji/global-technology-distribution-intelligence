## 1. Parent-project position

The project identifies useful existing technology providers, zero-capital commercial partnership routes, and trusted distribution networks that can connect solutions to relevant markets in Nigeria and beyond.

It is not currently a software-build project. First investigate existing solutions and partnership, integration, implementation, referral, reseller or distribution opportunities. Build only if a genuine gap and concrete demand are established.

The first detailed sector tracks are agriculture and mining. They remain separate. The parent scope is broader than either sector or any one local pilot.

- Do not repeat completed work. Revisit only for new evidence, material change, contradiction or necessary verification.
- Compare alternative providers; do not depend on Farmonaut or any single provider.

- Distinguish evidence, provider claims, independent findings, hypotheses and unknowns.
- Treat networks as unverified leads until identity, authority, active reach and activation capacity are checked.
- Do not contact, email, call, register, submit forms, pay or share data without explicit user authorisation.

- Keep the parent project separate from its sector and geographic branches.
