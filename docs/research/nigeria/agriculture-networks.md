# Nigeria Agriculture Distribution Network Leads

## Scope

Initial network leads relevant to agricultural technology and farm intelligence, particularly satellite crop monitoring and farm advisory. This is a preliminary map, not a list of confirmed partners.

## 1. Coalition of Farmers Association for Food Security in Nigeria (COA-FARMERS)

- Website: https://coalitionoffarmers.org.ng/
- Reported role: umbrella coalition with member organisations including Rice Farmers Association of Nigeria, Maize Association of Nigeria, Poultry Association of Nigeria, Nigeria Cassava Growers Association, and Ginger Growers Association.
- Potential relevance: could offer access to multiple commodity and farmer constituencies through a federated route.
- Key validation: current leadership, member organisations and their activity, geographic reach, decision authority, communication channels, and willingness to host or facilitate technology education.
- Status: **Unverified lead**.

## 2. National Agricultural Mechanization Cooperative of Nigeria (NAMCON)

- Website: https://namcon.org/
- Previously surfaced contact: info@namcon.org; 08092939313; 08033489287. Reconfirm before use.
- Reported role: collaboration involving farmers, cooperatives, development partners, and agricultural businesses.
- Potential relevance: cooperative and mechanisation relationships may provide routes to organised farmer groups and service providers.
- Key validation: current operating footprint, active cooperatives, leadership, authority, technology interests, and partnership process.
- Status: **Unverified lead**.

## 3. All Farmers Association of Nigeria (AFAN)

- Website: https://www.afanigeria.org/
- Previously surfaced description: national footprint across 36 states and the FCT, with state coordinators, field officers, and volunteers.
- Potential relevance: possible state-level access to farmer groups.
- Caution: multiple websites/portals have used the AFAN name, including a separate registration portal that advertised a fee. Confirm recognised leadership, official identity, and the correct channel; do not pay a registration fee as part of this project.
- Key validation: current legitimate national and state leadership, active state structures, membership evidence, and access procedures.
- Status: **Unverified lead; identity/leadership validation required**.

## 4. Plateau Commodity Market Company (PLACOM)

- Website: https://placommarkets.com/
- Previously surfaced contact: info@placom.ng. Reconfirm before use.
- Reported role: commodity marketing, supply-chain management, farmer cooperative development, and market access in Plateau State.
- Potential relevance: regional entry point connecting commodity value chains and organised producers.
- Key validation: current operations, actual cooperative relationships, commodities/geographies covered, and ability to introduce or convene farmers.
- Status: **Unverified lead**.

## Preliminary research priority

1. COA-FARMERS — potential multi-association access.
2. NAMCON — cooperative/mechanisation ecosystem.
3. AFAN — potentially broad geographic access, subject to identity and leadership validation.
4. PLACOM — possible regional route.

Priorities are hypotheses based on apparent fit, not verified assessments of current reach or activation ability.
