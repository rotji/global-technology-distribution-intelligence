# Nigeria Mining Distribution Network Leads

## Scope

Initial network leads relevant to mining and geospatial/mineral intelligence technology. This is a preliminary ecosystem map, not a list of confirmed partners.

## 1. Miners Association of Nigeria (MAN)

- Website: https://www.minersassociationofnigeria.ng/
- Earlier research described MAN as an umbrella body spanning exploration, mining, quarrying, mineral trading, and consultancy, with chapters across states and the FCT. This reach claim requires current corroboration.
- Potential relevance: direct access to mining businesses and operators.
- Previously surfaced contact from an older association site: info@minersassociationofnigeria.org.ng; +234 803 723 2643. Reconfirm through a current official source before use.
- Key validation: current leadership, chapter activity, member composition, and authority over partnerships.
- Status: **Unverified lead**.

## 2. Nigerian Society of Mining Engineers (NSME)

- Website: https://nsme.org.ng/
- Earlier research reported more than 900 members and a Jos zonal chapter covering Plateau, Bauchi, Gombe, Borno, Yobe, Adamawa, and Taraba. Both claims require revalidation.
- Potential relevance: technical practitioners, mining operators, professional credibility, and a possible regional entry point.
- Previously surfaced contact details: WhatsApp 07069352083; Secretary General 08029597732. Reconfirm before use.
- Key validation: current leadership, active chapter, membership, and ability to introduce operators or qualified practitioners.
- Status: **Unverified lead**.

## 3. Nigerian Mining and Geosciences Society (NMGS)

- Website: https://www.nmgs.org.ng/
- Earlier research described NMGS as an umbrella for mining and geoscience professional groups. A sector event source previously claimed a membership exceeding 10,000; this is not independently confirmed here.
- Potential relevance: geoscientists, researchers, public/private professionals, and technical credibility.
- Key validation: current membership and chapter activity, relevant member segments, current leadership, and formal partnership process.
- Status: **Unverified lead**.

## 4. Council of Nigerian Mining Engineers and Geoscientists (COMEG)

- Website: https://comeg.gov.ng/
- Earlier research described COMEG as the statutory professional regulator for geology, geophysics, geochemistry, mining engineering, and related professions.
- Potential relevance: not a conventional sales channel; may help identify the regulated professional ecosystem and qualified practitioners. Do not assume the regulator endorses commercial products.
- Key validation: current statutory remit, public registers, professional groups, and appropriate lawful engagement routes.
- Status: **Unverified ecosystem/intelligence lead**.

## Network behind the network: Nigeria Mining Week

Earlier research surfaced an event ecosystem involving MAN, NMGS, NSME, the Ministry of Solid Minerals Development, NEITI, PwC Nigeria, and Africa Finance Corporation. Event organisers and conveners may create cross-network introductions. This requires further research into current organisers, access mechanisms, event programming, and the availability/cost of relevant participation. Do not assume an event route is zero-cost.

## Preliminary priority

- MAN: potential route to operating mining businesses.
- NSME: potentially useful technical and regional practitioner route.
- NMGS: broader professional and geoscience ecosystem.
- COMEG: regulatory/professional ecosystem intelligence, not a presumed sales channel.

These are provisional priorities only. Validate identity, present leadership, current activity, actual access, and commercial suitability before ranking them definitively.
