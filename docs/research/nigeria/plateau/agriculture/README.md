# Plateau Agriculture Opportunity

## Position in the research hierarchy

This is a sector-specific child pathway under [Plateau State Economic & Opportunity Discovery](../README.md). The state economy remains the parent scope; agriculture is one part of that wider landscape, not a substitute for it.

This child research area examines Plateau's agricultural ecosystem, including agricultural activities and value chains as well as the wider systems that influence them: communities, people, institutions, research and laboratories, laws and public policy, standards, knowledge and information networks, finance, infrastructure, technology, markets, environmental conditions and adjacent sectors.

## Governing ecosystem architecture

Use the [Plateau Agriculture Ecosystem Architecture](ecosystem-architecture.md) as the broad topic map and organising framework. It is intentionally wider than farming and commodity value chains. It includes research institutions and laboratories, law and policy, standards and certification, data and information networks, infrastructure, finance and external influences that may appear peripheral but can materially shape agriculture.

The architecture defines **what to examine**, not **what has been found**. Its topic categories are not claims that particular organisations, programmes, facilities, relationships, problems or opportunities exist in Plateau State.

## Research posture

- Explore broadly across the 17 LGAs and relevant communities, ecological areas, watersheds, markets and value chains before committing narrowly.
- Do not assume that maize, one crop, one community, formal cooperatives, one institution, one technology or one information channel defines Plateau agriculture.
- Fadan Fobur and earlier maize-related work may provide useful starting evidence, but neither is the permanent centre of this research pathway.
- Examine both direct agricultural activity and the wider systems that influence it, including research, science, laboratories, law, policy, regulation, standards, public information, media, data, finance and adjacent sectors.
- Trace relationships between domains only when evidence supports them; a possible connection remains a research question until verified.
- Investigate existing solutions, providers and local practices before proposing a new product or service.

## Documentation standard

GitHub is the repository for durable, evidence-backed knowledge and governing research architecture—not a dumping ground for unverified leads.

- Document ecosystem principles, scope, topic categories and research questions without presenting them as local facts.
- Add factual findings only when an appropriate source directly supports the exact claim and its geographic and time scope.
- Use authoritative primary sources for laws, policies, institutional mandates, programme status, scientific capabilities and official data wherever possible.
- Distinguish an announcement from implementation, a stated allocation from disbursement, a facility from operational capacity, and a listed membership from active reach.
- Do not promote rumours, uncorroborated reports, speculative relationships or unverified opportunities into factual repository records.
- Keep unresolved claims in the active research process or working notes until they meet the evidence gate. If useful, document the question to investigate without repeating the unverified claim as fact.
- Preserve source dates, exact scope, limitations and what the evidence actually establishes. Revisit an earlier finding only when new evidence, a material change, contradiction or necessary validation justifies it.

## Research boundaries

This README is an index and scope definition, not a claim that all Plateau agriculture has been researched. The ecosystem architecture is a topic map, not a factual inventory. Add detailed research records only when verified evidence and a clear research purpose justify preserving them. Link to existing records rather than duplicating them.

No outreach, registration, payment, commitment, pilot, data collection from third parties or external representation is authorised by this research area alone.
