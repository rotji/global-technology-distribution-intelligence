# Plateau Agriculture Opportunity

## Position in the research hierarchy

This is a sector-specific child pathway under [Plateau State Economic & Opportunity Discovery](../README.md). The state economy remains the parent scope; agriculture is one part of that wider landscape, not a substitute for it.

This child research area examines Plateau's agricultural activities, value chains, participants, needs, resources, constraints, existing practices, technologies, services and commercial relationships. It should remain open to different crops, livestock, production systems, processing, storage, aggregation, markets and supporting services as evidence warrants.

## Research posture

Do not assume that maize, one crop, one community, formal cooperatives, one institution or one technology provider defines Plateau agriculture. Compare relevant agricultural activities and market systems before prioritising a particular opportunity.

Fadan Fobur and earlier maize-related work may provide useful starting evidence, but neither is the permanent centre of this research pathway.

## Existing research records

- [Plateau maize pilot market selection](../../plateau-maize-pilot-market-selection.md) — an earlier, bounded maize-market hypothesis. Retain its historical context and do not generalise its findings to all agriculture or all LGAs.
- [Nigeria agriculture networks](../../agriculture-networks.md) — cross-state network research that may contain relevant leads; confirm Plateau-specific reach rather than assuming it.
- [Nigeria agriculture provider and use-case comparison](../../agriculture-provider-use-case-comparison.md) — cross-state technology/provider assessment; its technical and commercial findings should not be treated as proof of Plateau demand or local adoption.

## Research questions

Depending on the evidence and the specific question, investigate:
- Which agricultural activities and value chains are present in the locations being studied?
- Who produces, supplies, aggregates, processes, transports, buys, finances or provides services?
- What are the actual constraints, losses, coordination problems and unmet needs?
- What practices, local solutions, technologies and service providers are already used?
- Which buyers, budget owners or institutions could have a reason to pay for a solution?
- Could an existing provider or operator address the need through partnership, implementation, integration or distribution?
- What local skills, equipment, materials, infrastructure and relationships could enable a practical response?
- What evidence is missing, and what would justify a deeper investigation?

## Evidence discipline

Use the parent project's [Opportunity Discovery Methodology](../../../../methodology/opportunity-discovery-methodology.md) and [Evidence and Verification](../../../../methodology/evidence-and-verification.md).

Keep source dates, geographic scope, confidence, unknowns and verification status explicit. Do not infer statewide conditions from a single community, study sample, programme announcement or provider case study.

## Boundaries

This file is an index and scope definition, not a claim that all Plateau agriculture has been researched. Add detailed dated records when there is a concrete research question and evidence to preserve. Link to existing records rather than duplicating them. No outreach, registration, payment, pilot commitment or external representation is authorised by this research area alone.
