## How to use this register

Update an LGA's status only when the research record supports the change. A suggested progression is:

- **Not yet assessed:** no structured discovery pass recorded.
- **Initial contextual leads only:** a few references or leads exist, but coverage is narrow or not verified.
- **Initial discovery recorded:** multiple dimensions have been explored and dated findings captured; this does not mean comprehensive coverage.
- **Comparative review completed:** findings have been compared with other relevant LGAs or sectors for a defined question.
- **Targeted deep dive justified:** a specific question or opportunity warrants deeper research, with the reason recorded.

These are workflow labels, not quality scores. Add a date and link to the underlying research record when changing a status.

## Minimum discovery note for an LGA

For each research pass, capture only what evidence supports:
- Research date and geographic scope.
- Communities or settlements examined, with boundary/administrative uncertainty noted.
- Economic activities and value chains observed or documented.
- Businesses, institutions, organisations and informal actors identified.
- Infrastructure, resources, skills and service capabilities relevant to the question.
- Problems, constraints, existing practices and local solutions.
- Technologies and service providers already present or available.
- Potential opportunity hypotheses and who may benefit or pay.
- Source references, evidence confidence and unresolved questions.
- Safety, access and verification considerations.
- Related records and the reason for any deeper investigation.

Do not infer statewide coverage from a single community, sample, event, organisation or study.

## Comparative discipline

Compare like with like where possible, and explain differences where they are not comparable. Do not rank LGAs by unsupported impressions or incomplete data. Record coverage gaps and uncertainty explicitly. A low number of documented leads may reflect limited research rather than a lack of activity.

## Relationship to sector records

This register tracks geography, not every detail. Keep detailed sector, provider, institution or thematic findings in their appropriate research records and link them here. Do not duplicate large sections of content across LGA entries.
