# Farmonaut — Company Profile

## Role in the project

Farmonaut is the initial technology-provider case study. Agriculture and mining are evaluated as separate commercial and market tracks.

## Product and capability claims collected

The user's website research captured claims about:
- Satellite-based crop monitoring and crop-stress detection using red and near-infrared wavelengths.
- Field-level information relating to crop health, irrigation/water, soil parameters, yield estimates, pest and disease risk, and fertiliser needs.
- JEEVN AI farm advisory with six panels.
- Optical and radar satellite observations and analytics.
- Field boundary drawing or upload, browser access, PDF reports, shareable links, and broad language support.
- A mining-related satellite mineral-detection offering.
- API services and subscription/pricing information.

These are a summary of website material supplied by the user. They are not an independent technical audit or proof of product performance in Nigeria.

## Website metrics

The material supplied by the user included Farmonaut website figures such as 975,000+ acres monitored, 107,000+ fields mapped, 135 countries, and 166 crops, alongside other business/customer/user figures. Treat all such numbers as provider self-reported marketing claims until independently corroborated. Different figures may refer to different products, periods, or definitions.
