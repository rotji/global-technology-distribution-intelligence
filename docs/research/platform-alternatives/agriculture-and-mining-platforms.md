# Technology Platform Alternatives: Agriculture and Mining

**Research snapshot:** 2026-11-09  
**Status:** Preliminary desk research; provider capabilities and partnership claims are primarily provider-published and have not been independently tested.  
**Purpose:** Build a multi-provider portfolio so the project does not depend on Farmonaut alone.

## 1. Strategic decision

Farmonaut remains the first case study, not the exclusive provider or predetermined partner. Maintain separate provider comparisons for:

- **Agriculture:** crop/field monitoring, agronomic intelligence, production and supply-risk intelligence, APIs, white-label and reseller options.
- **Mining:** mineral prospectivity screening, remote-sensing interpretation, geophysical/subsurface intelligence, exploration targeting, and mine-planning workflows.

Do not treat every geospatial platform as a direct Farmonaut competitor. Some are adjacent tools, data infrastructure, enterprise platforms, or specialist services.

## 2. Agriculture alternatives

### EOS Data Analytics (EOSDA)
- **Overlap:** Satellite crop monitoring, vegetation indices, weather history, field analytics and API access.
- **Partner evidence:** EOSDA publicly advertises a global reseller programme for EOSDA Crop Monitoring, including onboarding, training, technical/marketing support and a dedicated account manager.
- **Commercial caution:** The published model describes partners purchasing a quantity of hectares and reselling/allocating them to customers. This may require working capital. Do not classify as zero-capital until EOSDA confirms whether a referral-only/no-prepurchase arrangement is available.
- **Assessment:** Strong functional comparator and a potentially useful channel relationship, subject to capital requirements.
- Sources: https://eos.com/partnership/ ; https://eos.com/user-guide/crop-monitoring/access-through-api/ ; https://eos.com/faq/

### Cropin
- **Overlap:** Enterprise agricultural intelligence combining field records, satellite, weather and climate data; crop/yield/supply prediction, crop risk, field workflows, APIs and integrations.
- **Partner evidence:** Cropin publicly identifies solution, affiliate, technology and strategic partnership routes. Its partner proposition includes joint solutions, expert support, training, integration and revenue opportunities.
- **Commercial caution:** The public material is oriented toward enterprise solutions and strategic partners. It does not establish that an individual Nigerian referrer can join without a registered business, technical capability or commercial commitments.
- **Assessment:** Potentially broader and more enterprise-oriented than Farmonaut's farmer-facing monitoring proposition; worth qualifying for institutional, insurer, lender, processor and government use cases.
- Sources: https://www.cropin.com/partners/ ; https://www.cropin.com/partner-with-us/ ; https://www.cropin.com/platform/

### OneSoil
- **Overlap:** Satellite field monitoring, NDVI/crop-health monitoring, anomaly detection, weather, field comparison and precision-agriculture tools.
- **Partner evidence:** OneSoil positions itself as a growth partner for agri-service companies and offers a white-label partner contact route. Its help centre says prospective OneSoil Pro dealers should contact support; it describes existing dealers across multiple countries.
- **Commercial caution:** Public pages do not establish individual Nigerian eligibility, referral commission, payout terms, or whether a dealer must buy licences/services.
- **Assessment:** A relevant agriculture alternative, especially for agronomists, machinery dealers, input suppliers and agri-service businesses.
- Sources: https://onesoil.ai/en ; https://onesoil.ai/en/platform ; https://onesoil.ai/en/contact-us?form=white_label_partner ; https://help.onesoil.ai/en/articles/5237693-what-is-onesoil

### Farmonaut
- **Overlap:** Satellite crop monitoring and farm-management intelligence, with separate mining services and software.
- **Partner evidence:** Current published agriculture track advertises 20% recurring commission and a self-serve affiliate dashboard. Mining is a manually reviewed, project-based partner programme with commission agreed individually.
- **Commercial caution:** Confirm Nigeria eligibility, payout mechanics, attribution and programme conditions directly. Mining programme is not open self-serve enrolment.
- **Assessment:** Keep as one candidate, not the default choice.
- Sources: https://farmonaut.com/partner ; https://farmonaut.com/faqs ; https://farmonaut.com/farmonaut-affiliate-program/mining/

## 3. Adjacent geospatial / location-intelligence platforms

These are not direct substitutes for farm monitoring, but could support other intelligence products or provide technology for partners serving agriculture, mining, infrastructure and other sectors.

### Aspectum
- GIS, mapping, spatial analytics and business intelligence.
- Publicly advertises a reseller programme for local agencies and a referrer route for GIS professionals, analysts and individual business consultants.
- Its page describes industry-level commission, product support, free trials and marketing support, but does not specify Nigerian eligibility or exact commercial terms.
- Assessment: A relatively promising partner lead for the project's zero-capital screening, but a different product category from crop monitoring.
- Source: https://aspectum.com/resellers-program/

### CARTO
- Enterprise location intelligence for spatial analysis, site selection, territory management, geomarketing and mobility analysis.
- Public partner programme includes referral-only through OEM agreements, with dedicated resources for joint opportunities.
- Assessment: More likely a formal enterprise/SI or specialist channel relationship than a simple affiliate offer. Could be valuable for a future location-intelligence service.
- Sources: https://carto.com/partners/ ; https://carto.com/partners

## 4. Mining alternatives and adjacent providers

Mining is more fragmented than agriculture. Farmonaut's mining offering combines satellite-derived mineral prospectivity with reporting and, in Mining Pro, browser-based workflows for target ranking, 3D visualisation, drillhole handling, conceptual mine planning and economics. Other providers may solve only part of that workflow.

### Madini
- **Overlap:** Africa-focused satellite mineral prospectivity mapping for gold, copper, lithium and silver.
- Public product description says it combines Sentinel-2, ASTER, EMIT hyperspectral and elevation data, applies deposit-model weighting, and produces ranked targets, PDF reports, GeoTIFF rasters and GeoJSON polygons.
- **Partner evidence:** No public partner programme identified in this research pass.
- **Assessment:** Important Africa-focused technical alternative to evaluate, especially for Nigerian exploration areas. Commercial partnership route remains unknown.
- Source: https://madini.ai/

### TerraEye
- **Overlap:** Mineral exploration analysis and targeting using multiple evidence types and geological interpretation.
- A recent comparison article describes TerraEye as a platform/service combining exploration analysis, targeting and geological support. The article is written by TerraEye and is therefore not independent validation.
- **Partner evidence:** Not established.
- **Assessment:** Candidate for technical and commercial due diligence, not yet a qualified partner lead.
- Source: https://blog.terraeye.co/mineral-exploration-software-and-services-compared-choosing-the-right-tools-for-your-next-program/

### VRIFY
- **Overlap:** AI-supported mineral prospectivity mapping and targeting, combining exploration datasets and geoscience expertise.
- **Partner evidence:** Not established in this research pass.
- **Assessment:** Specialist mining-intelligence candidate; investigate product access, geographic coverage, customer profile and channel policy.
- Source: TerraEye comparative article above; direct VRIFY product verification still required.

### Marigold by EarthDaily
- **Overlap:** Satellite mineral mapping, structural tools and public geophysical data for remote-sensing analysis.
- **Partner evidence:** Not established.
- **Assessment:** Specialist remote-sensing alternative; investigate direct product evidence and partnership route.
- Source: TerraEye comparative article above; direct provider verification still required.

### Fleet Space / ExoSphere
- **Overlap:** Subsurface mineral exploration intelligence, but not a direct satellite-only substitute. ExoSphere combines satellite communications, ground sensors, ambient-noise tomography and AI to build 3D geological models.
- **Partner evidence:** Not established in this research pass.
- **Assessment:** A different and potentially complementary technology layer. Likely relevant to exploration companies with budgets for field/sensor programmes, rather than a simple referral affiliate comparison.
- Source: https://www.theaustralian.com.au/business/companies/fleet-space-technologies-has-raised-a-huge-amount-to-further-its-minerals-exploration-goals/news-story/5cb355e3daf287895973227818a5769f

### Important technical boundary
Satellite-derived prospectivity outputs are exploration-screening evidence, not proof of an economic deposit or a compliant Mineral Resource/Reserve. Ground mapping, sampling, geophysics, drilling, assays and qualified-person review remain necessary as appropriate to the project stage. Do not market remote-sensing targets as confirmed discoveries.

## 6. Standard qualification questions for every provider

1. Can an individual based in Nigeria become a referral, affiliate, reseller or channel partner?
2. Is a registered company required?
3. Is there any joining fee, deposit, minimum purchase, licence, equipment, inventory or paid certification?
4. Can the relationship be referral-only, with the provider handling technical work, contracting, billing and customer support?
5. What commission or margin applies, how is it calculated, and is it recurring or one-off?
6. What payout methods, currencies, minimum thresholds and payment schedules are available in Nigeria?
7. How are leads registered, attributed and protected?
8. Can the partner work through associations, cooperatives, consultants, distributors and other network owners?
9. Are sub-referrals, co-branded campaigns, white-label arrangements or revenue sharing with network owners permitted?
10. What customer evidence, case studies and technical limitations should be disclosed?

## 7. Research rules and next work

- Keep agriculture and mining as separate comparison tracks.
- Compare providers against the same customer problem and use case; do not compare marketing feature lists as if products were identical.
- Distinguish product capability from independently validated performance.
- Distinguish a public partner page from confirmed eligibility or agreed terms.
- Do not assume a programme is zero-capital until all required purchases and costs are confirmed.
- Do not send outreach or submit applications unless the user asks.
- Preserve Farmonaut as the initial case study while building a multi-provider shortlist.

**Recommended next sequence:**
1. Agriculture: compare Farmonaut, EOSDA, Cropin and OneSoil against the same practical Nigerian use cases and partnership conditions.
2. Mining: compare Farmonaut, Madini, TerraEye, VRIFY, Marigold/EarthDaily and Fleet Space by the exploration decision each supports.
3. Then select a small number of providers for deeper due diligence and possible partnership enquiries.
