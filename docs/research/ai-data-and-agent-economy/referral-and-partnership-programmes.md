## Common questions
- Can an individual based in Nigeria participate?
- Is a registered company required?
- Can an independent participant source companies through public research without an existing personal network?
- Are international company referrals accepted?
- What exact event earns compensation?
- What are the commission formula, cap, recurring terms and payment timing?
- What attribution, lead registration and first-touch rules apply?
- What payout methods, currencies, thresholds, tax forms and withholding apply to Nigerian residents?
- Is a formal agreement required, and what confidentiality, non-circumvention, exclusivity, liability or compliance obligations apply?
- Is a decision-maker introduction or existing relationship required?

## Evidence and decision rules
- Official programme pages establish what a provider publicly advertises, not necessarily full contractual terms.
- Written provider confirmation is required for Nigerian eligibility, international sourcing, exact compensation and payout.
- A programme remains **Conditional** until material commercial and operational terms are confirmed.
- Do not submit a lead until attribution rules and the user's role are understood.
- Do not represent a company as interested, qualified or authorised without its permission.
- Do not pay fees or disclose data to unlock a promised commission.
- A large advertised reward is not evidence of probability of success or expected income.
