# AI Data, API and Agent Opportunity Assessment

## Purpose
Provide a consistent decision screen for opportunities discovered in the AI data and agent economy. Use proportionately; do not assign numerical scores when key evidence is missing.

## Assessment dimensions

| Dimension | Questions |
|---|---|
| Real demand | What specific problem or requirement exists? Who experiences it? |
| Buyer and authority | Who uses, approves, procures and pays? |
| Evidence | What is confirmed, self-reported, inferred or unknown? |
| Existing alternatives | Which providers, marketplaces, services or manual processes already address it? |
| Differentiation | What meaningful capability or access remains unavailable? |
| Rights and compliance | Who owns the data and can authorise use? What privacy, security and contractual rules apply? |
| Access from Nigeria | Can an individual or appropriately structured business participate? |
| Zero-capital fit | Are fees, purchases, deposits, inventory, licences or paid certification required? |
| Economics | What is the payment trigger, amount, recurrence, costs and expected effort? |
| Payment | Are payout method, currency, threshold, timing and tax requirements workable? |
| Operational capability | What skills, tools, reliability, support and delivery obligations are required? |
| Dependencies | Does the route depend on one platform, buyer, provider or data source? |
| Risk | What legal, reputational, security, financial or execution risks exist? |
| Next decision | What missing evidence would change the decision, and what is the smallest justified next step? |

## Decision outcomes
- **Continue research:** relevant questions remain and further evidence is justified.
- **Conditional:** promising but material requirements remain unconfirmed.
- **Qualified:** demand, authority, rights, eligibility, economics and operating requirements are sufficiently evidenced.
- **Defer:** not actionable under current constraints or timing.
- **Reject:** evidence shows the route is unsuitable or fails a required condition.
- **Monitor:** no immediate action; a defined external change may make it relevant.

## Zero-capital filter
A route is not zero-capital merely because registration is free. Record unpaid labour, travel, connectivity, equipment, paid tools, certification, compliance, liability, support and time-to-payment. Avoid paying fees to unlock promised commissions.

## Build gate
A new product or platform is not justified by technical feasibility alone. Establish a specific material customer problem; a buyer or concrete customer request/incentive; a genuine gap after comparing existing alternatives; lawful durable data access; a feasible delivery model; proportionate costs and risks; and explicit user approval.

## Evidence rule
Every material conclusion must link to evidence and its date. Provider marketing claims, advertised reward amounts, public programme descriptions and project hypotheses must remain distinct from written provider confirmation, independently corroborated facts and actual outcomes.
