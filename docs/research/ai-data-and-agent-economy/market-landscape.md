# AI Data and Agent Economy — Market Landscape

**Research status:** Exploratory synthesis  
**As-of date:** 21 November 2026

## Value chain
The AI data economy includes data ownership and generation; discovery and sourcing; rights verification and licensing; collection, cleaning, normalisation and anonymisation; annotation and expert feedback; model evaluation and red teaming; dataset delivery; and API/software/agent access to data or specialist capabilities.

A company may operate across several stages. A referral intermediary, data broker, marketplace, data supplier and data-preparation service are distinct roles.

## Who may pay
Potential payers include AI model developers, AI application companies, enterprise AI teams, research organisations, data platform operators and businesses procuring specialist information or services.

The party using an AI agent is not necessarily the party paying for the underlying data. Payment and authorisation generally depend on the application operator, developer, enterprise customer or contracting party. An agent or protocol does not itself establish a budget or purchase authority.

## Distinct business models

| Model | Value delivered | Main question |
|---|---|---|
| Data owner/licensor | Grants defined data-use rights | Who owns it and what rights can be granted? |
| Data sourcing partner | Finds and qualifies data owners/assets | What counts as accepted sourcing and how is it paid? |
| Marketplace | Helps buyers discover and transact | Does it buy, broker, list or license? |
| Broker/intermediary | Connects supply and demand | Who contracts and bears rights risk? |
| Data preparation provider | Collects, cleans, annotates or structures | What quality and delivery obligations apply? |
| Evaluation/expert-data provider | Supplies expertise, feedback or testing | What skills and security requirements apply? |
| API/MCP provider | Exposes data or functions programmatically | Who authorises, meters and pays for usage? |
| Intelligence service | Produces verified information or monitoring | What recurring decision is valuable enough to fund? |

## Possible independent entry routes
- Introduce qualified data owners under a written referral programme.
- Source and qualify assets with owner authority and documented rights.
- Become an approved supplier or service provider after satisfying capability and compliance requirements.
- Provide research, verification, monitoring or data preparation under a defined contract.
- Distribute or integrate existing APIs and specialist services under permitted terms.
- Eventually expose a differentiated, rights-cleared intelligence capability through API/MCP if demand justifies it.

Each route has different barriers. Supplier routes may require a registered business, security controls, operational capacity or rights documentation. API products may require reliable data, engineering, support, customer acquisition and ongoing costs.

## Questions before prioritising
1. Who is the buyer and who controls the budget?
2. What specific data, expertise, workflow or outcome is purchased?
3. Who owns the data and can authorise licensing?
4. What quality, provenance, coverage, freshness and delivery standards apply?
5. What legal, privacy, confidentiality and security controls are required?
6. Can a Nigerian individual participate?
7. What is the exact compensation trigger and payment route?
8. What work, costs, liability and obligations fall on the participant?
9. Which existing providers already solve the problem?
10. Is there repeatable demand, rather than only a promotional claim?
