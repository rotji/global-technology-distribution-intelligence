## Reply-handling protocol
1. Identify sender, company, date and whether the message relates to the inquiry.
2. Preserve the original response and distinguish explicit answers from assumptions.
3. Classify each point as provider-confirmed, unclear, conditional or unanswered.
4. Check Nigerian eligibility, international referrals, company requirements, sourcing method, qualifying event, commission, attribution, payout and legal terms.
5. Prepare a concise follow-up for missing material points.
6. Show the proposed reply to the user.
7. Send only after the user explicitly approves the exact message and recipient.

## Status vocabulary
- **Email sent:** user confirms inquiry was sent.
- **Acknowledged:** provider confirms receipt.
- **Reply received:** provider has sent a response, not necessarily a substantive answer.
- **Provider-confirmed:** provider explicitly confirms a specific point in writing.
- **Conditional:** a material condition remains.
- **Qualified:** key eligibility, commercial, payout and operational requirements are sufficiently evidenced.
- **Deferred:** no current action; may be revisited.
- **Rejected:** evidence shows the route is unsuitable or incompatible.

Update this tracker only when a message or user-confirmed action supports a status change. Do not infer a reply from an automation run, read receipt or absence of a response. Record dates from the actual email. No reply has been represented as received in this update.
