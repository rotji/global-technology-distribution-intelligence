# AI Data, API & Agent Economy

## Purpose
Investigate the commercial ecosystem around data used by AI systems, specialist data and intelligence services, APIs, MCP and AI-agent workflows. The objective is to identify legitimate, evidence-backed ways an independent operator based in Nigeria could participate without upfront capital, while understanding longer-term opportunities for specialist services and data products.

This is a research pathway under Global Technology & Distribution Network Intelligence. It does not replace the parent mission and does not authorise a product build, third-party data collection or entry into a commercial programme.

## Scope
- AI data demand and supply: ownership, sourcing, preparation, annotation, evaluation, licensing and delivery.
- Data marketplaces, brokers, sourcing partners and service providers.
- Independent sourcing and referral programmes.
- Data rights, authority, consent, privacy, confidentiality, security and provenance.
- API and MCP access, authorisation, metering, distribution and monetisation.
- Specialist intelligence services such as research, verification, monitoring and decision support.
- Practical access from Nigeria, no-upfront-cost conditions, payment routes and operating requirements.

## Research rules
- Separate provider marketing claims from written confirmation, independent evidence and actual outcomes.
- Do not assume that an individual can sell, license or refer data merely because a platform has a supplier page.
- Public accessibility does not automatically grant commercial reuse or AI training rights.
- Establish the actual data owner and authority to grant rights.
- Do not upload samples, disclose confidential information, sign terms, register as a supplier or contact additional organisations without the user's approval.
- Advertised rewards are not guaranteed income.
- Verify Nigerian eligibility, payment method, thresholds, tax treatment, attribution and qualifying events.
- Prefer existing platforms and infrastructure before proposing a new product.
- API and MCP are access mechanisms, not proof of demand or automatic payment channels.
- Preserve the zero-upfront-capital constraint unless the user explicitly changes it.

## Current research position — 21 November 2026
The work has progressed from general data-marketplace exploration into a focused examination of data sourcing and referral programmes.

Three inquiries have been sent:
- Patronus AI — awaiting response.
- micro1 — awaiting response.
- Turing / Project Lazarus — awaiting response.

Scale AI and Handshake AI were researched but deferred; no inquiry is recorded as sent to either.

No Nigerian eligibility, payout route, commission entitlement or successful referral outcome has yet been confirmed for the three active inquiries. See the programme comparison and outreach tracker.

The project is also examining DataFactor, Defined.ai and Data Partners HQ as examples of different data sourcing, supplier and intermediary models. Their models should not be conflated with referral programmes.

API/MCP and specialist intelligence opportunities remain exploratory. No product build or commercial demand has been approved.

## Research sequence
1. Understand the AI data and agent value chain and identify who pays whom.
2. Compare marketplaces, brokers, suppliers, data-preparation providers and referral intermediaries.
3. Establish the data assets and rights required for each model.
4. Verify individual eligibility, geography, compensation, attribution and payment.
5. Examine API/MCP distribution and identify actual buyers, budgets and use cases.
6. Compare routes against zero-capital, skills, time, legal and operational constraints.
7. Prioritise only when material evidence supports a practical next step.
