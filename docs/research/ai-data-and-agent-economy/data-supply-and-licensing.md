# Data Supply and Licensing

**Research status:** Foundational research framework  
**As-of date:** 21 November 2026

## Rights come first
Technical usefulness does not establish that a person or company has the right to sell, license, disclose or submit a dataset. Data sourcing must begin with ownership, authority and permitted use.

## Asset categories
- Publicly released information and open datasets.
- Company operational records and internal workflow data.
- Private software repositories and engineering records.
- Customer, employee, supplier or transaction records.
- Audio, video, image, text and multimodal material.
- Expert-created content and domain-specific knowledge.
- Synthetic/generated data.
- Research, scientific and geospatial datasets.

These categories have different legal, contractual, privacy, confidentiality and quality requirements.

## Rights and authority checklist
1. Identity of the actual owner or rights holder.
2. Authority of the person offering the asset.
3. Relevant contracts, licences, contributor agreements and third-party restrictions.
4. Presence of personal, sensitive, confidential or regulated information.
5. Required consent, notice, lawful basis, anonymisation or safeguards.
6. Permitted purpose, model-training rights, redistribution, sublicensing and geography.
7. Exclusivity, duration, termination, retention and deletion conditions.
8. Provenance, collection method, dates, coverage, quality and limitations.
9. Security, transfer, storage and access controls.
10. Whether evaluation samples can be shared and under what agreement.

Do not infer rights from public accessibility, possession of a copy, employment, repository access or willingness to discuss a dataset.

## Private code and engineering data
Potential records include source code, commit history, pull requests, issues, reviews, tests, CI logs, documentation and engineering decisions. Before commercial use, investigate ownership, contributor rights, client/employer contracts, open-source licences, secrets, credentials, personal information and confidentiality.

Public repository visibility does not automatically grant rights to resell or license it as a commercial dataset. Private repository data requires especially careful authority and contractual review.

## Licensing is not always a sale
A transaction may grant limited data-use rights rather than transfer ownership. Terms can specify purpose, model training, internal evaluation, derivative works, sublicensing, retention, geography, exclusivity, security and deletion.

## Sample-sharing caution
A supplier may be asked to share samples to evaluate quality or gauge buyer interest. Sample terms can grant the platform or prospective customers rights to view or use the sample. Read the applicable terms before uploading anything. Do not submit confidential or personal data without authority and safeguards.

## Project rule
No data sample, private repository, company record or personal information is to be collected, uploaded, disclosed or offered to a third party as part of this research without explicit user approval and a documented rights and permission check.
