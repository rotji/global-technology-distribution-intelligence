# Partnership Models

## Models to investigate

| Model | How it works | Key verification |
|---|---|---|
| Affiliate | Trackable referral link or code earns a defined commission | Eligibility, tracking, attribution, payout, permitted promotion |
| Referral partner | Submit qualified leads or introductions | Lead registration, acceptance criteria, fee trigger, payment |
| Reseller | Sell provider's product or subscription, often with a margin | Authorisation, pricing, customer support, purchase commitments |
| Distributor/channel partner | Access a defined market or customer segment | Territory, exclusivity, targets, training, minimums, support |
| Strategic/institutional partnership | Provider and institution cooperate on education, access, or adoption | Authority, responsibilities, benefits, branding, data and liability |
| Consultant/integrator | Recommend or implement the solution as part of professional services | Competency requirements, certification, implementation liability |
| Co-branded programme | Provider and intermediary offer a joint member/customer programme | Branding approval, member value, economics, operational ownership |

## Questions common to all models

1. Can an individual based in Nigeria participate?
2. Is a registered company required?
3. Is there any fee, deposit, mandatory purchase, inventory, paid certification, or equipment requirement?
4. Is referral-only participation possible?
5. What event triggers compensation: click, lead acceptance, subscription, payment, renewal, or completed project?
6. Is the earning one-off or recurring?
7. What are the commission, fee, or reseller margin terms?
8. What is the attribution window and how are disputes handled?
9. What are the payout methods, currencies, minimum thresholds, and payment schedule?
10. Are institutional referrals, co-branded campaigns, or shared commissions allowed?
11. Can the partner use sub-agents or share commissions with an independent network owner?
12. Who handles sales, onboarding, technical support, refunds, and complaints?

## Preferred initial route

Start with the lowest-friction permitted route, ideally a no-fee individual referral arrangement. Develop institutional or network-level distribution only after the provider confirms that this is allowed and the intermediary's role and compensation are clear.

## Commercial integrity

Do not present estimated revenue as a guaranteed outcome. Record the exact terms, source, date, and whether they were confirmed in writing.
