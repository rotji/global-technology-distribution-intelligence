# Zero-Capital Approach

## Objective

Find legitimate routes to earn through technology partnerships without requiring the project operator to fund inventory, licences, equipment, fees, or mandatory paid training upfront.

## Pass/fail screen

An opportunity should be deferred from the initial zero-capital shortlist if it requires:
- Registration or onboarding fees.
- A compulsory product or subscription purchase.
- Inventory or stockholding.
- Paid software licences or equipment.
- Mandatory paid training or certification.
- A minimum sales commitment that creates material financial exposure.

A free programme can still impose substantial unpaid labour, technical obligations, or customer-support costs. Record those separately.

## Essential checks

- Individual eligibility from Nigeria.
- Whether a company registration is mandatory.
- Whether the opportunity is genuinely no-fee.
- Whether a referral-only tier exists.
- Commission or margin and qualifying event.
- Payout route accessible from Nigeria.
- Currency, threshold, payment timing, and withholding.
- Tracking and attribution rules.
- Tax, consumer-protection, privacy, and sector-specific considerations.
- Permission to work through associations or other network owners.

## Risk discipline

Do not pay to unlock a promised commission. Do not rely on screenshots, informal claims, or an affiliate landing page alone for material terms. Obtain written provider confirmation and inspect the programme agreement.

## Decision statuses

- **Pass:** evidence supports a no-upfront-cost route and material terms are sufficiently clear.
- **Conditional:** promising, but one or more material requirements remain unconfirmed.
- **Defer:** requires capital or capabilities not currently available.
- **Reject:** unsuitable, misleading, or incompatible with project constraints.
