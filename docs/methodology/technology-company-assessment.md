# Technology Company Assessment Methodology

## Purpose

Assess whether a provider's product solves a real problem and whether a legitimate, commercially accessible partnership can connect it to a viable customer group.

## Company profile

Record:
- Company name and official website.
- Product(s) and product category.
- Main problems solved and evidence of capability.
- Target users, buyer, budget owner, and decision-maker.
- Geography and language support.
- Deployment and infrastructure requirements.
- Pricing model and purchasing route.
- Customer evidence, case studies, and independent validation.
- Risks, dependencies, and alternatives.

## Partnership profile

Record:
- Programme name and official terms URL.
- Model: affiliate, referral, reseller, channel, institutional, or other.
- Eligibility and geographic restrictions.
- Individual/company requirements.
- Fees, purchases, inventory, certification, or sales targets.
- Compensation and qualifying event.
- Recurring or one-time nature.
- Tracking and attribution.
- Payout method, currency, minimum, and timing.
- Network distribution permissions.
- Support, responsibilities, and termination conditions.

## Market fit

Assess:
- Is the underlying problem present in the target market?
- Is the product suitable for local infrastructure, language, regulation, and purchasing power?
- Who experiences the problem most acutely?
- Who has budget and authority to buy?
- What proof would a customer need before purchase?
- Which existing network already serves that customer group?

## Decision

Use **Qualified**, **Conditional**, **Deferred**, or **Rejected**. State evidence, uncertainty, key risks, and the next validation needed. Do not assign a definitive score when core facts are unknown.
